Name of the research project:
Targeted delivery of antitumor and diagnostic agents based on conjugates with ligand proteins, nanoparticles, and natural compounds
Project team:
Professor, D.Sc. Feldman N.B. PhD student: Savina V.A.
The project is carried out jointly with:
Shemyakin–Ovchinnikov Institute of Bioorganic Chemistry of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Orekhovich Research Institute of Biomedical Chemistry, Sechenov University, Lomonosov Moscow State University.

 

Project description:
The project integrates fundamental and applied research in the field of targeted delivery of biologically active compounds. Conjugates of antitumor drugs (doxorubicin, esperamicin A1), photosensitizers (phthalocyanines, chlorins), and nanoparticles (agaricic acid nanoparticles) with ligand proteins — alpha-fetoprotein and epidermal growth factor — are studied. The cytotoxic and antitumor activity of the obtained conjugates, their accumulation, and intracellular distribution in sensitive and resistant tumor cells are investigated. A separate direction is devoted to the study of redox signaling, the structural organization of bacterial cellulose, and the biological activity of agaricic acid nanoparticles.
Project goal:
Development and study of targeted delivery systems for antitumor and diagnostic agents based on conjugates with ligand proteins, nanoparticles, and natural compounds to improve the efficacy of therapy and overcome drug resistance of tumor cells.
̵	Key objectives:
̵	Synthesis and characterization of doxorubicin conjugates with epidermal growth factor and its receptor-binding fragment.
̵	Study of the cytotoxic activity, accumulation, and intracellular distribution of anthracycline antibiotics and their conjugates in sensitive and resistant MCF-7 cells.
̵	Investigation of the antitumor activity of alpha-fetoprotein conjugates with doxorubicin and esperamicin A1 in vitro and in vivo.
̵	Study of the cytotoxic action of alpha-fetoprotein and epidermal growth factor conjugates with photosensitizers.
̵	Assessment of the biological activity of agaricic acid nanoparticles against human hepatoma HepG2 cells.
̵	Study of redox signaling and adaptive redox switches through oxidative and nitrosative protein modifications.
̵	Investigation of the structural organization of bacterial cellulose.

Name of the research project:
Cell-free translation systems for bioengineering and synthetic biology
Project team:
PhD Khabibullina N.F. Students: Dzagashtova D., Odnolko P., Oleinik N., Palamarchuk D.
The project is carried out jointly with:
Lomonosov Moscow State University, Shemyakin–Ovchinnikov Institute of Bioorganic Chemistry of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Research Institute of Systems Biology and Medicine of Rospotrebnadzor.
 
Project description:
The project is aimed at bioengineering developments and optimization of cell-free expression systems for the production of protein products. Using methods of genetic engineering, bioengineering, and synthetic biology, the capabilities of cell-free translation systems in biotechnology and the production of non-protein products are investigated.
Project goal:
Bioengineering development and optimization of cell-free expression systems for the efficient production of protein products and non-protein compounds.
Key objectives:
̵	Development and optimization of cell-free expression systems for the production of target protein products.
̵	Application of genetic engineering methods to improve the components of cell-free translation systems.
̵	Investigation of the capabilities of cell-free translation systems in biotechnology.
̵	Study of the potential of cell-free systems for the production of non-protein products.
̵	Integration of synthetic biology approaches to expand the functionality of cell-free expression systems.

Name of the research project:
Development and study of bacteriophages, endolysins, and dosage forms for infection therapy and tissue regeneration
Project team:
Professor, D.Sc. Anurova M.N. Master's students: Zapevalov A.T., Nizova A.R. Students: Drozd A.A.
The project is carried out jointly with:
Pirogov Russian National Research Medical University, Mechnikov Research Institute of Vaccines and Sera, Sechenov First Moscow State Medical University, Gamaleya National Research Centre of Epidemiology and Microbiology.

 
Project description:
The project integrates fundamental and applied research in the field of phage therapy, the development of recombinant endolysins, and the creation of new dosage forms for the treatment of infectious diseases and stimulation of tissue regeneration. Lytic bacteriophages (Cutibacterium acnes, Salmonella), their genomic characterization, and therapeutic potential are studied. Endolysin-containing gels for the treatment of anaerobic infections are being developed, and their pharmacokinetics and safety are investigated. A separate direction is devoted to the development and study of new dosage forms: dental films with ketorolac, eye gels with interferon, suppositories with bacteriophages, intranasal forms of naloxone, as well as the study of the wound-healing activity of dihydroquercetin.
Project goal:
Development and comprehensive study of bacteriophages, recombinant endolysins, and innovative dosage forms for the therapy of antibiotic-resistant infections, as well as for applications in dentistry, ophthalmology, and regenerative medicine.
Key objectives:
•	Isolation and genomic characterization of lytic bacteriophages Cutibacterium acnes and Salmonella.
•	Study of the therapeutic potential of bacteriophages for the treatment of antibiotic-resistant intestinal infections.
•	Development and study of the pharmacokinetics and safety of an endolysin-containing gel for topical application.
•	Evaluation of the efficacy of an endolysin-containing antibacterial gel for the treatment of anaerobic infection caused by Fusobacterium necrophorum.
•	Determination of the bactericidal activity spectrum of recombinant endolysins of bacteriophages ECD7, Am24, Ap22, Si3, and St11.
•	Analysis of the pharmacokinetics of suppository forms of bacteriophages.

Name of the research project:
Bacterial cellulose: structure, biomedical materials, and biotechnological platforms based on it.
Project team:
PhD Gavryushina I.A. PhD student: Kravchenko E. Student: Balayan M.
Project goal:
Comprehensive study of the structure and properties of bacterial cellulose and the creation of biomedical materials and biotechnological platforms based on it for cell immobilization and the production of biologically active compounds.
The project is carried out jointly with:
Lomonosov Moscow State University, Sechenov University, National Research Centre "Kurchatov Institute".

   

Project description:
The project integrates fundamental and applied research on bacterial cellulose (BC) synthesized by Gluconacetobacter hansenii. The structural organization of BC, the origin of anisotropy and layered structures are studied, and methods for purifying BC membranes using supercritical media are being developed. BC-based biomedical materials are created with assessment of their cytotoxicity for eukaryotic cells, as well as matrices for the immobilization of Escherichia coli cells, Fomitopsis officinalis mycelium, and Laetiporus sulphureus culture. The antimicrobial properties of the obtained extracts are investigated, and biotechnology for their production is being developed.
Project goal:
Comprehensive study of the structure and properties of bacterial cellulose and the creation of biomedical materials and biotechnological platforms based on it for cell immobilization and the production of biologically active compounds.
Key objectives:
̵	Study of the structural organization of BC, the origin of anisotropy and layered structures.
̵	Development of new approaches to the purification of BC membranes, including supercritical media.
̵	Creation of BC-based biomaterials for medical applications and assessment of their cytotoxicity.
̵	Development of BC matrices for the immobilization of Escherichia coli cells.
̵	Obtaining immobilized mycelium of Fomitopsis officinalis — a producer of agaricic acid.
̵	Development of biotechnology for obtaining immobilized Laetiporus sulphureus culture on BC and study of the antimicrobial properties of its extracts.

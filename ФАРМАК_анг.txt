Project team: к.х.н. Апполонова С.А., аспиранты Резванов П.А., Болдин А.А.
Project description:
Objective: Development of digital platforms for: preclinical trial management, organotoxicity prediction, drug bioequivalence studies, and pharmacotherapy monitoring.



Key points:
-Metabolomic study of drug-induced organotoxicity using zebrafish as a model organism;
-Investigation of changes in the plasma metabolomic profile of patients;
-Creation of databases for assessing drug-induced organotoxicity using metabolomic biomarkers;
-Development of web applications for pharmacokinetic analysis in preclinical drug studies;

The name of the research project: «Development of protective strategies for drug-induced hearing loss»
Project team: Associate Professor, Ph.D Bakaeva Z.V., Assistant professor Polikarpov E.V., student Orlov A.S.
Project description: 
Objective: Investigation of the mechanisms of ototoxicity and identification of the otoprotective properties of natural compounds, with the aim of developing new strategies for the prevention and treatment of drug-induced sensorineural hearing loss. 

The work is being conducted in collaboration with the Laboratory of Neurobiology and Fundamental Principles of Brain Development at the National Medical Research Center for Children's Health (Ministry of Health of Russia), the Institute of General Pathology and Pathophysiology, and the Institute of Gene Biology of the Russian Academy of Sciences (IGB RAS).

Key points:

-Investigation of the molecular mechanisms of hair cell damage induced by ototoxic drugs;
-Development of new approaches to the pharmacological screening of potentially ototoxic agents;
-Identification and evaluation of the otoprotective effects of natural and synthetic compounds with antioxidant activity;

The name of the research project: “Study of the mechanisms of neuroprotection and neuroregeneration”
Project team: Associate Professor, Ph.D. Bakaeva Z.V., PhD students Zgodova A.E., Chernova S.S., students Lizunova N.V., Kislukhina E.N., Boyarkin D.P., Alekseev V.I.
Project description: 
Objective: Investigation of the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying the neuroprotective and neuroregenerative effects of biologically active compounds following nervous tissue injury, aimed at identifying promising pharmacological approaches for the treatment of cerebral ischemia and neurotrauma.
The project is being conducted jointly with the Laboratory of Neurobiology and Fundamental Principles of Brain Development at the National Medical Research Center for Children's Health (Ministry of Health of Russia), Lomonosov Moscow State University, the Institute of General Pathology and Pathophysiology, and the Institute of Gene Biology of the Russian Academy of Sciences.
Key points:                                                                                        
– Investigation of the neuroprotective activity of neuropeptides, neurolipins, and flavonoids in in vitro models of glutamate excitotoxicity, hypoxia, and neuronal injury;
– Study of the neuroregenerative potential of the test compounds in primary neuroglial cultures using a mechanical injury model (scratch assay);
– Investigation of the molecular and cellular mechanisms of neuroprotection, including alterations in calcium homeostasis, mitochondrial functional status, and intracellular signaling pathways;
– In vivo evaluation of the neuroprotective and neuroregenerative potential of promising compounds by using experimental models of ischemic brain injury, employing histology, immunohistochemistry, and wide-field optical neuroimaging.

 Research Project Title: “‘PARKINSON_NET_INFO’ Chatbot - a Digital Information Support System for Stakeholders in Parkinson's Disease Therapy”
Project Team: Professor of the Department, Doctor of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Professor Glembotskaya G.T.; Assistant, PhD student Kristeleva D.A.; Doctor of Medical Sciences, Professor of the Department of Nervous Diseases Shindryaeva N.N.
Project Description:
Project Aim: to develop the digital chatbot “PARKINSON_NET_INFO” providing information support to physicians, pharmacy professionals and patients with Parkinson's disease and aimed at improving the quality of pharmacotherapy and medicines supply using modern digital technologies.
Key Objectives:
- to develop a framework for digital information support for physicians, pharmacy professionals and patients with Parkinson's disease;
- to ensure that patients receive timely information on pharmacotherapy and medicines supply;
- to develop tools for collecting and assessing data on changes in patients' condition over time;
- to support the identification of patients at risk of cognitive impairment to enable timely referral and follow-up;
- to provide information support to physicians in pharmacotherapy assessment and medication selection;
- to enable improvement of the recording and monitoring of antiparkinsonian medication dispensing and the analysis of medicines supply;
- to develop approaches to integrating the “PARKINSON_NET_INFO” chatbot into the digital healthcare infrastructure, including the electronic medical record, the patient portal and telemedicine services;
- to identify prospects for implementation and commercialization of the solution in healthcare organizations and digital healthcare environments.

Research Project Title: “Calculator for Forecasting Costs and Consumption Volume of Antiepileptic Therapy”
Project Team: PhD, Associate Professor Zakharova O.V., PhD, Associate Professor Vaskova L.B.; PhD student Pozdnyakov G.A.
The project is carried out at the Department of Organization and Economics of Pharmacy of the A.P. Nelyubin Institute of Pharmacy, Sechenov First Moscow State Medical University (Sechenov University), in collaboration with the Kozhevnikov Clinic of Nervous System Diseases of University Clinical Hospital No. 3, Sechenov University.
Project Description:
Project Aim: development of an automated management decision support tool for planning the medicines supply for patients with epilepsy that forecasts costs and consumption volume of antiepileptic medicines at the healthcare organization level, taking into account the clinical and demographic characteristics of the patient cohort.
Key Objectives:
- development of a multivariable regression model of pharmacotherapy costs based on clinical and demographic predictors of patients with epilepsy.
- implementation of the calculation of pack requirements and costs using the ATC/DDD methodology of the World Health Organization;
- development of a mechanism for automatic re-estimation of the regression model as new data accumulate at the inpatient stage of care;
- assessment of forecast accuracy using the coefficient of determination (R²), mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) and confidence intervals;
- procurement volume planning for antiepileptic medicines.

Research Project Title: “Improving Access to Biologic Therapy through Digital Solutions”
Project Team: PhD, Associate Professor of the Department of Organization and Economics of Pharmacy Gerasimova D.A., students: Kolesova A.I., Lisenkov A.D., Chinareva V.A.
The project is carried out jointly with the V.A. Nasonova Research Institute of Rheumatology.
Project Description:
Project Aim: to improve the accessibility of biologic therapy for patients with chronic diseases by optimizing the dosing and allocation of biologics.
Key Objectives:
- to conduct a clinical and economic analysis of biologics for the treatment of systemic autoimmune rheumatic and oncological diseases;
- to develop an algorithm for individualized dose selection of biologics depending on the patient's body weight/body surface area;
- to propose a quantitative model for calculating the total number of packs of biologics for procurement by a healthcare organization;
- to develop a dose optimization program using machine learning algorithms and digital platforms.

Research Project Title: “‘Information Exchange in the Provision of Pharmaceutical Care to Pregnant Women with ARVI’ Ecosystem”
Project Team: PhD, Associate Professor Gribova Ya.V., Lecturer Andrun A.S., student: Pershina A.R.
Project Description:
Project Aim: to develop the PC software “PharmHelpMamaARVI” based on an optimal algorithm for pharmaceutical counseling of pregnant women with acute respiratory viral infections (ARVI), which will increase the confidence of pharmacists and the quality of the pharmaceutical care provided through modern digital technologies.
Key Objectives:
•	based on a literature review, to analyze the current state and the regulatory framework of the organization of pharmaceutical care for pregnant women with ARVI at the outpatient stage in the Russian Federation and worldwide;
•	to conduct a comprehensive analysis of the assortment of medicines for the provision of outpatient care to pregnant women with ARVI;
•	to study the factors shaping the medical and demographic profile of a pregnant woman with ARVI and to determine her consumer behavior;
•	to identify the specific features of pharmaceutical counseling of pregnant women with ARVI based on a sociological survey of participants in pharmaceutical care (obstetrician-gynecologist – pharmacist – pregnant woman);
•	to develop an algorithm for pharmaceutical counseling of pregnant women with ARVI and a technical specification for the computer program “PharmHelpMamaARVI” in order to automate pharmaceutical counseling;
•	to develop guidelines for pharmaceutical counseling of pregnant women with ARVI.

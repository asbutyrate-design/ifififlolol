The name of the scientific project: «Study of the adsorption capacity of pharmaceutical enterosorbents»

   Project team: Associate professor, candidate of chemical sciences Zhukova A.A., students: Malysheva E., Glukhova E., Markelova A., Besedina A.
Project description:
The enterosorption method is actively used in the treatment of acute and chronic intoxications of various origins, which are increasingly common in medical practice. Understanding the adsorption activity of enterosorbents allows for a more reasonable choice of treatment regimens and drugs, which, in turn, contributes to more effective therapy results. The results of the study make it possible to give an objective assessment of the effectiveness of existing drugs and optimize the choice of sorbent for specific clinical situations.
Key tasks:
- Study of physico-chemical properties of pharmaceutical enterosorbents of various compositions.
- Conducting a comparative analysis of the morphology, structure, and surface composition of selected enterosorbents using optical microscopy and IR spectroscopy. 
- Conducting a qualitative analysis of the adsorption activity of selected enterosorbents by visual colorimetry with various low molecular weight toxins.
- Carrying out a quantitative analysis of the adsorption activity of selected drugs by acid-base titration, reverse titrimetry and photoelectrocolorimetry

The name of the scientific project: «Improving the bioavailability of poorly soluble medicinal products – solid dispersions in medicine and pharmacy»
Project team: head of the department, PhD, professor Krasnyuk I.I. (Jr.); candidate of pharmaceutical sciences, associate professor Grik V.V.; candidate of pharmaceutical sciences, associate professor Grigorieva V.Yu.; candidate of pharmaceutical sciences, associate professor Slonskaya T.K.; candidate of pharmaceutical sciences, associate professor Evgrafov A.A.; assistant Stantsov M.I.; postgraduate students: Terskikh K.A., Shifrin M.A., Bubnova M.A.
The project is carried out jointly with the department of pharmaceutical technology of the of institute of pharmacy, Sechenov First Moscow State Medical University (Sechenov University) and the Laboratory of pharmaceutical technologies at the scientific and production site of the Center for Regenerative Medicine, Lomonosov Moscow State University
Project description:
Project goal: to develop innovative medicinal products (including fast dissolving ones) containing solid dispersions (SD) of low solubility active pharmaceutical substances (APS) with improved biopharmaceutical properties.
Key tasks:
- screening of low solubility APS in water and optimal carrier polymers for preparing SD;
- development of technology for producing SD of the studied APS;
- development of the composition and technology for producing innovative medicinal products using previously developed SD;
 - standardization of the obtained preparations, assessment of bioavailability, stability, and shelf life during storage.

The name of the scientific project: «The quality of spraying pharmaceutical aerosols and sprays»
                                                      
    Project team: associate professor, сandidate of pharmaceutical sciences V.G. Yankova; associate professor, candidate of chemical sciences S.V. Gribanova; associate professor, candidate of pharmaceutical sciences V.Yu. Grigorieva; associate professor, candidate of pharmaceutical sciences I.L. Udyanskaya; students: D. Pasivkina, I. Obruchkova, M. Lutkova, S. Konon, M. Makarenko.                                                                                                                                             
The project is carried out jointly with the S.S. Kutateladze Institute of Thermophysics of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences (Novosibirsk). 
Project description:
Based on a comparative analysis of the key parameters of spray and aerosol formulations of nasal medications and remedies for treating sore throat, select the most effective drug in terms of delivering the medicinal substance to the mucous membrane of the throat.
Key tasks:
1. Study of the spraying quality of spray and aerosol formulations based on the following indicators: reproducibility and uniformity of the dose mass, the area of the irrigated surface on a planar simulation model, the area of the irrigated surface on a silicone nasal model.
2. Study of the dynamic characteristics of spraying and the particle size distribution of nasal medications and throat pain treatments using shadow photography.

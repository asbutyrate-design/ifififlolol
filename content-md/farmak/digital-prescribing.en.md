## Project description

## Objective

Development and evolution of the unified "Digital Formulation" digital platform, integrating training, simulation exercises, professional information sources, and skill mastery assessment into a single licensed product..

## Key points

- Personalization of training;
- Personalization of pharmacotherapy;
- Integration into the digital healthcare environment;
- Creation of a digital platform combining training, simulation exercises, professional information sources, and skills mastery assessment into a single licensed product;
- Development and implementation of a supplementary professional education program;

# Development of digital platforms for drug research and development

Project team: к.х.н. Апполонова С.А., аспиранты Резванов П.А., Болдин А.А.

## Project description

Objective: Development of digital platforms for: preclinical trial management, organotoxicity prediction, drug bioequivalence studies, and pharmacotherapy monitoring.

## Key points

-Metabolomic study of drug-induced organotoxicity using zebrafish as a model organism;
-Investigation of changes in the plasma metabolomic profile of patients;
-Creation of databases for assessing drug-induced organotoxicity using metabolomic biomarkers;
-Development of web applications for pharmacokinetic analysis in preclinical drug studies;

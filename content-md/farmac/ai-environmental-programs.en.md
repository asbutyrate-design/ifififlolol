# Artificial Intelligence in the System of Promoting Environmental Programs in Pharmaceutical Organizations

Project team: Doctor of Medical Sciences, Professor Babaskin D.V., students: Voronin A.N., Ilycheva E.E.

## Project description

Project goal: optimization of the system for promoting environmental programs in pharmaceutical organizations through the use of artificial intelligence technologies.

## Key objectives

- conducting an analysis of scientific literature data on the current state of the use of artificial intelligence in promoting environmental programs;
- providing empirical substantiation of the need to optimize the system for promoting environmental programs in pharmaceutical organizations;
- conducting a marketing analysis of the factors determining the possibilities of using artificial intelligence to optimize environmental programs in pharmaceutical organizations and their interactions (SWOT analysis);
- developing practical recommendations for optimizing the system for promoting environmental programs in pharmaceutical organizations using artificial intelligence;
- writing and submitting an article to a Scopus or VAK journal.

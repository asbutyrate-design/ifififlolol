# Drug Development Strategy Based on the Quality by Design (QbD) Concept

## Project Team

Project Lead: E.O. Bakhrushina, Doctor of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Professor
Project Team: M.M. Shumkova, PhD in Pharmaceutical Sciences; I.B. Mikhel; V.S. Pyzhov; P.S. Sakharova; E.V. Kashevarova, Resident; A.A. Ananyan, PhD Student.

## Project Description

## Project Objective

To systematically develop a drug development strategy based on the Quality by Design (QbD) concept, including the establishment of the Quality Target Product Profile (QTPP), identification of Critical Quality Attributes (CQA), assessment of Critical Material Attributes (CMA) and Critical Process Parameters (CPP), establishment of the Design Space, and development of a control strategy to ensure reproducibility and consistent quality of the finished product throughout its lifecycle.

## Key Objectives

- Develop a methodological framework for applying the QbD concept in pharmaceutical development, integrating QTPP, CQA, CMA, CPP, and Design Space into a unified development strategy.
- Establish the Quality Target Product Profile (QTPP) and identify Critical Quality Attributes (CQA), with scientifically justified relationships between quality attributes and the safety and efficacy of the drug product.
- Assess Critical Material Attributes (CMA), including characterization of the physicochemical properties of active pharmaceutical ingredients, analysis of the functional role of excipients, and investigation of API–excipient compatibility.
- Optimize formulation composition and process parameters using Design of Experiments (DoE) and develop quantitative formulation–process–quality models.
- Conduct risk analysis using FMECA and other methodologies to identify critical factors affecting product quality and develop a risk management strategy.
- Establish the Design Space and define the Normal Operating Range (NOR) to support scale-up and change management.
- Develop an integrated control strategy covering raw materials, manufacturing processes, and the finished product to ensure consistent product quality.

## Expected Results

- A scientifically substantiated methodology for applying QbD in pharmaceutical development, suitable for implementation across different dosage forms.
- Established approaches to QTPP development and CQA identification, including acceptance criteria and scientific justification.
- Systematized data on the assessment of CMA and CPP for various groups of drug products, including targeted drug delivery systems.
- Optimized formulations and manufacturing process parameters developed using DoE, supported by quantitative models and an established Design Space.
- A risk-based control strategy, including risk maps, justification of critical control points, and risk management measures.
- A comprehensive QbD documentation package suitable for inclusion in a regulatory dossier.

# Pharmaceutical Development of High-Technology Immunotropic Drug Products

Project Team: Doctor of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Professor Smirnov V.V.; Turenko V.N.; Bagaev D.E.; Bello Taye; Spiridonov A.V.; Dolgov D.S.

## Project Description

Project Aim: To conduct the pharmaceutical development of drug products based on small interfering RNA (siRNA) and recombinant allergens (allergy vaccines) for the treatment of immune system diseases.

## Key Objectives

- Development of purification and characterization protocols for synthetic siRNA intermediates — the sense and antisense oligonucleotide strands;
- Development of approaches to the standardization and quality control of siRNA-based candidate drug products, taking into account the characteristics of the delivery systems employed, the chemical modifications of the oligonucleotide sequences, and the dosage form;
- Investigation and optimization of the pharmacokinetic profile of siRNA-based candidate drug products;
- Preparation of synthetic linear peptides reproducing IgE epitopes of allergens, and assessment of their immunogenicity;
- Selection of a carrier protein and production of recombinant allergy vaccines;
- Optimization of quality control methods and development of specifications for the recombinant allergy vaccines produced.

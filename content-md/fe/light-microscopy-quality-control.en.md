# Use of light microscopy in the quality control of medicinal plant raw materials and medicinal plant preparations

Project team: Dr. Pharm. Sci., Associate Professor Bobkova N.V., postgraduate student: Pham Phuong Nam, students: Diana Bayramkulova, Darya Gadetskaya.

## Project description

The goal of the project is to study the capabilities of various types of light microscopy (bright field, luminescent, polarizing, etc.) in the identification and quality control of medicinal plant raw materials and medicinal plant preparations (MPPs).

## Key tasks

- Studying the influence of technological characteristics on the microscopic analysis of medicinal plant raw materials of various morphological groups.
- Development of sample preparation methods for microscopic analysis of MPPs and solid dosage forms based on medicinal plant raw materials.
- Study of anatomical and diagnostic features of related species of medicinal plants.
- Use of luminescent and polarizing microscopy in the analysis of medicinal plant raw materials and medicinal products. Development of sample preparation methods.
- Study of the possibility of using microchemical and histochemical reactions in the analysis of medicinal plant raw materials and MPPs.

# Glycans of Medicinal Plant Raw Materials

Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Associate Professor D.O. Bokov
The project is carried out at the Department of Pharmaceutical Natural Sciences, Institute of Pharmacy named after A.P. Nelyubin, Sechenov University.

## Project Description

## Project Goal

Development, optimization, and validation of modern methods for the qualitative and quantitative analysis of glycans in medicinal plant raw materials, herbal medicinal products, and herbal mixtures to improve the accuracy, selectivity, and reproducibility of pharmaceutical analysis.

## Key Objectives

‒ Development of a method for the quantitative determination of plant glycans by spectrophotometry using the anthrone reagent.
‒ Investigation of the polysaccharide complex (in particular, the qualitative and quantitative evaluation of inulin) by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) using appropriate detectors.
‒ Detailed profiling of simple carbohydrates (mono- and oligosaccharides) using capillary electrophoresis, which ensures high separation efficiency of complex matrix components.
‒ Metrological evaluation and validation of the developed methods (assessment of linearity, accuracy, and intralaboratory precision) for subsequent implementation into current regulatory documentation.

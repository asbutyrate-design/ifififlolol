## Project description

## Project goal

to develop innovative medicinal products (including fast dissolving ones) containing solid dispersions (SD) of low solubility active pharmaceutical substances (APS) with improved biopharmaceutical properties.

## Key tasks

- screening of low solubility APS in water and optimal carrier polymers for preparing SD;
- development of technology for producing SD of the studied APS;
- development of the composition and technology for producing innovative medicinal products using previously developed SD;
 - standardization of the obtained preparations, assessment of bioavailability, stability, and shelf life during storage.

# Study of the adsorption capacity of pharmaceutical enterosorbents

## Project team

Associate professor, candidate of chemical sciences Zhukova A.A., students: Malysheva E., Glukhova E., Markelova A., Besedina A.

## Project description

The enterosorption method is actively used in the treatment of acute and chronic intoxications of various origins, which are increasingly common in medical practice. Understanding the adsorption activity of enterosorbents allows for a more reasonable choice of treatment regimens and drugs, which, in turn, contributes to more effective therapy results. The results of the study make it possible to give an objective assessment of the effectiveness of existing drugs and optimize the choice of sorbent for specific clinical situations.

## Key tasks

- Study of physico-chemical properties of pharmaceutical enterosorbents of various compositions.
- Conducting a comparative analysis of the morphology, structure, and surface composition of selected enterosorbents using optical microscopy and IR spectroscopy.
- Conducting a qualitative analysis of the adsorption activity of selected enterosorbents by visual colorimetry with various low molecular weight toxins.
- Carrying out a quantitative analysis of the adsorption activity of selected drugs by acid-base titration, reverse titrimetry and photoelectrocolorimetry

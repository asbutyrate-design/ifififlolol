# Cell-free translation systems for bioengineering and synthetic biology

## Project team

PhD Khabibullina N.F. Students: Dzagashtova D., Odnolko P., Oleinik N., Palamarchuk D.

## The project is carried out jointly with

Lomonosov Moscow State University, Shemyakin–Ovchinnikov Institute of Bioorganic Chemistry of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Research Institute of Systems Biology and Medicine of Rospotrebnadzor.

## Project description

The project is aimed at bioengineering developments and optimization of cell-free expression systems for the production of protein products. Using methods of genetic engineering, bioengineering, and synthetic biology, the capabilities of cell-free translation systems in biotechnology and the production of non-protein products are investigated.

## Project goal

Bioengineering development and optimization of cell-free expression systems for the efficient production of protein products and non-protein compounds.

## Key objectives

- Development and optimization of cell-free expression systems for the production of target protein products.
- Application of genetic engineering methods to improve the components of cell-free translation systems.
- Investigation of the capabilities of cell-free translation systems in biotechnology.
- Study of the potential of cell-free systems for the production of non-protein products.
- Integration of synthetic biology approaches to expand the functionality of cell-free expression systems.

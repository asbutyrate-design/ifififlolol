# Development and study of bacteriophages, endolysins, and dosage forms for infection therapy and tissue regeneration

## Project team

Professor, D.Sc. Anurova M.N. Master's students: Zapevalov A.T., Nizova A.R. Students: Drozd A.A.

## The project is carried out jointly with

Pirogov Russian National Research Medical University, Mechnikov Research Institute of Vaccines and Sera, Sechenov First Moscow State Medical University, Gamaleya National Research Centre of Epidemiology and Microbiology.

## Project description

The project integrates fundamental and applied research in the field of phage therapy, the development of recombinant endolysins, and the creation of new dosage forms for the treatment of infectious diseases and stimulation of tissue regeneration. Lytic bacteriophages (Cutibacterium acnes, Salmonella), their genomic characterization, and therapeutic potential are studied. Endolysin-containing gels for the treatment of anaerobic infections are being developed, and their pharmacokinetics and safety are investigated. A separate direction is devoted to the development and study of new dosage forms: dental films with ketorolac, eye gels with interferon, suppositories with bacteriophages, intranasal forms of naloxone, as well as the study of the wound-healing activity of dihydroquercetin.

## Project goal

Development and comprehensive study of bacteriophages, recombinant endolysins, and innovative dosage forms for the therapy of antibiotic-resistant infections, as well as for applications in dentistry, ophthalmology, and regenerative medicine.

## Key objectives

- Isolation and genomic characterization of lytic bacteriophages Cutibacterium acnes and Salmonella.
- Study of the therapeutic potential of bacteriophages for the treatment of antibiotic-resistant intestinal infections.
- Development and study of the pharmacokinetics and safety of an endolysin-containing gel for topical application.
- Evaluation of the efficacy of an endolysin-containing antibacterial gel for the treatment of anaerobic infection caused by Fusobacterium necrophorum.
- Determination of the bactericidal activity spectrum of recombinant endolysins of bacteriophages ECD7, Am24, Ap22, Si3, and St11.
- Analysis of the pharmacokinetics of suppository forms of bacteriophages.
